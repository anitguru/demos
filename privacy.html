An IT Guru video-upload — privacy policy

This application ("video-upload") is a personal tool written by its developer to publish videos to their own YouTube channels.

  • It requests only the YouTube upload scope, used to upload video files the developer creates.
  • No data is collected, stored, shared, or sold. OAuth tokens are stored only on the developer's own machine.
  • Only the developer's own Google and brand accounts use this application.
